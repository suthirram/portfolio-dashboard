3 · Why XIRR answers "did I time it well?"

XIRR weighs each amount by how long it was invested. Move the second purchase and watch the rate change even though the price path is identical. This runs a port of the backend's xirr() (Newton–Raphson, ACT/365).

Price path

₹100 a share on 1 Jan 2025, ₹200 from 1 Jul 2025 to today (1 Jan 2026).

XIRR
—

The first ₹1,000 is fixed on 1 Jan 2025. A second buy before July catches the doubling, one after it does not: the dashboard's P/L % treats both the same, XIRR does not.
