Investment delta from the transactions ledger

The All transactions page's investment delta panel (PD-050) now computes each month from the ledger: every holding's buys and sells replayed as of the month end, the cost basis summed per currency, and the change from the previous month end. The super admin can switch an account back to the History-based version. The chart, its columns and its year arrows do not change.

About these frames

This page plays the Figma role (docs/visuals/README.md); colours and sizes come from frontend/src/styles/tokens.css via the PD-050 spec. Every figure is computed live in the page from the sample ledger in frame 02, with a port of the server's replay (average cost, as of each day), so editing a trade there moves the chart in frame 01, the month table, and the API response in frame 04. History rows are simulated as snapshots on chosen days (healed, so they equal the ledger on the day they exist). Today is 3 Nov 2026. Example data; not production markup.
