Investment delta chart

A collapsible panel just below the Transactions page's filters, expanded on demand, charts each month's investment delta for one year: a green or red bar for the money added or sold, and a line for the invested amount at each month end. Each bar is the History month card's figure for that month.

About these frames

No Figma account is wired into this project, so this page plays that role (docs/visuals/README.md). Every colour, radius and type size is read from frontend/src/styles/tokens.css, and the chart follows the History charts' idiom. October 2026 uses the owner's real figures (+₹18,826.38, ₹8,98,847.70) and June 2025 Europe the owner's +€599.58; the other months are example data. Not production markup.

  1. 1Twelve bars, Jan to Dec. Each is that month's investment delta, the same figure as the History card for the month: October is 8,98,847.70 − 8,80,021.32 = +₹18,826.38. Green for money in, red for cost basis sold (March). December has no row yet, so no bar and no point. November is the current month: inside the live window, before today's snapshot lands, its bar and point include today's live row from live prices, the same row the History card reads (History's live-row logic, moved into the shared useLiveHistoryRows hook), so a buy made today shows at once and the month still equals the card. The line, on its own right-hand axis, is invested at each month end (₹8,98,847.70 for October): a month's delta is a small fraction of the total, so the two never share an axis. ‹ › change the year (› disabled on the current year); the switch offers only columns with money invested, Gold only for a gold-tracking user. After a transaction is added, edited or deleted in a holding's ledger from this page, the open panel reloads the year (the backend has healed the snapshots by then) with the chart kept on screen meanwhile; collapsed, it fetches nothing. PRD-007 §4–§5, DD-007 §3–§4.

  2. BLoading. The year loads when the panel opens and again on each year change; a late response for a year already left is dropped.
  3. CEmpty year. No History row dated in the year with money invested: no currency to offer, and the panel says so. The latest earlier row, which include_previous returns even for an empty year, is only January's opening, never data for the year. PRD-007 §5, DD-007 §4.
  4. DError. A failed load shows the message inside the panel; the rest of the page is unaffected.
  5. EFirst year. ‹ is disabled at 2020, the History picker's first year (MIN_YEAR).
