Month performance

A card at the top of History compares each currency's position, and gold's, on the previous month's last row with the month's latest row. It shows the P/L % at both ends, the change in percentage points, and an investment performance figure that leaves out money added during the month.

The four metrics

MetricFormulaIndia, October 2026
Opening P/L(current − invested) ÷ invested, last row before the month(10,86,274.39 − 8,80,021.32) ÷ 8,80,021.32 = 23.44%
Closing P/Lsame, on the month's latest row(11,18,139.13 − 8,98,847.70) ÷ 8,98,847.70 = 24.40%
P/L changeclosing − opening, as displayed, in pp; 🟢 up, 🔴 down, ⚪ flat24.40 − 23.44 = +0.96 pp
Performance*(closing value − opening value − money added) ÷ opening value; money added = closing invested − opening invested(31,864.74 − 18,826.38) ÷ 10,86,274.39 = +1.20%
